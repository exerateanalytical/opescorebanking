## Proposed operational requirements
1. **GOV-018-R01:** Assign legal/regulatory reviewer to effective dates, instrument scope, conflicting articles, licensure and finality authority.
2. **GOV-018-R02:** Assign CFO/ledger reviewer to monetary precision, double entry, postings, reversals, value dates, chart mappings, suspense and independent reconciliation.
3. **GOV-018-R03:** Assign security reviewer to least privilege, maker-checker, cryptographic/key custody, secrets, insider threat and tenant isolation.
4. **GOV-018-R04:** Assign AML/fraud reviewer to screening, monitoring, audit, immutable evidence and risk exception governance.
5. **GOV-018-R05:** Assign payments reviewer to participant entitlement, ISO 20022 profile, external state machines, unknown outcomes and settlement receipts.
6. **GOV-018-R06:** Assign operations reviewer to observability, capacity, backup-restore rehearsal, continuity, incident response and end-of-day cutover.
7. **GOV-018-R07:** Assign architecture reviewer to bounded-context ownership, API compatibility, event durability, data consistency and dependency graph.
8. **GOV-018-R08:** Assign accessibility and customer-operations reviewers to browser/mobile usability, localization, error clarity and recoverable staff workflows.
9. **GOV-018-R09:** Require evidence-based PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN/NOT_APPLICABLE with reviewer and artifact link; forbid fabricated executed tests.
