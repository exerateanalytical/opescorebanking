# Banking education curriculum and lab scenarios
Status: DRAFT; mapped to 25 commercial bounded contexts but all operations simulated.
## 12-course progression
1. Banking regulation and ethical conduct: fictional bank licensing perimeter, bank secrecy, consumer protection, maker-checker.
2. CIF/KYC/KYB: synthetic customers, identity evidence, beneficial ownership and review.
3. Banking product factory: deposits, fees, interest and effective-dated rules.
4. Branch/teller: drawer opening, deposits, withdrawals, dual custody, EOD.
5. Accounting: chart of accounts, journals, trial balance, correction and reconciliation.
6. Payments: initiation, authorization, clearing, settlement, return and unknown outcomes.
7. Credit: underwriting, collateral, disbursement, amortization, delinquency and restructuring.
8. Treasury: FX quote, liquidity buffers, nostro reconciliation and funding.
9. AML/fraud: screening, alert triage, escalation, confidentiality and evidence.
10. Regulatory reporting: reconcile source ledger and prepare mock return with provenance.
11. Digital banking/security: access control, channel limits, IDOR and incident exercises.
12. Audit/DR/capstone: restore a mock bank, reconcile data, close books and present findings.
## Capstone case
A simulated bank operates three branches for 30 virtual days. Students rotate roles. Instructor injects a cash shortage, duplicate payment request, false-positive sanctions alert, disputed credit repayment, FX rate change, interrupted payment rail and backup restoration. Required deliverables: audit trail, reconciled trial balance, case decisions, customer communication, supervisory report and root-cause review.
## Rubric
Accuracy 30%; controls and authorization 20%; reconciliation 20%; investigation 15%; documentation 10%; accessibility/communication 5%. Passing thresholds are institution-configured and require examiner approval; not automatically equivalent to professional certification.
## Assessment safeguards
Synthetic fixtures only; no self-grading; instructor moderation; pseudonymous analytics; accessible accommodations; instructor can inspect exact commands and journals; retention and student privacy controlled by institution.
