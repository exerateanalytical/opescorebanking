## Newly actionable architecture observations (proposed, not law)
1. **Numeric code identity is not semantic identity.** Historical 3211 means commercial discount; in the 2015 structure 3211 denotes commercial paper within healthy short-term credit, with discount at 32111.
2. **Branch resolution is not interbank finality.** 2002 Article 7 describes internal entity notices and liaison accounts; it does not prove a settlement instruction has cleared an external scheme.
3. **Unallocated off-balance code is not a license to create or hide money.** 2002 Article 18 describes 999 unmatched computer entries. Design requires independent suspense ownership, reconciliation, escalation and immutable financial accounting.
4. **Restricted merchandising accounts need institutional authority.** 2002 articles 8-13 explicitly restrict 473/640/740 usage to authorized credit institutions conducting direct sale of movable goods.
5. **Correspondent ratings are versioned reference data.** 2002 Article 19's historical COBAC 1–4 scale must not be hard-coded as a current prudential rule without verifying later changes.

## Limitations
The official catalogue lists I-2015/01 as its latest PCEC-labelled instruction, but catalogue silence is **not** proof that no later instrument, circular, clarification or institution-specific direction exists. No COBAC erratum, formal legal opinion, institution-approved consolidated chart, complete 01/25 or 02/25, Court disposition, or settlement approval was obtained.
