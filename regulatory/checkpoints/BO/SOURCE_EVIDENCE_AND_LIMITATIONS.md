# BO — Evidence acquisition and source ranking (2026-11-02)

## S1 — BEAC 2021 Financial Stability Review (issuer-hosted official PDF, secondary regulatory summary)
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/RSF_AC_2021.pdf
Section 3.1.2.6 (printed p. 37–38) expressly identifies COBAC R-2021/01 (amends R-2010/01) and R-2021/02 (amends R-2010/02). It reports that zero weighting may be available for certain state-issued securities where a secured repayment mechanism guarantees repayment and that conditions/methods were specified. **What is established:** amendment existence and conditionality as described by BEAC. **Not established:** complete binding clauses, precise eligibility test, current amendments, issuer authentication of the operative instrument, any institution/instrument approval, effective-date history. Do not convert summary into executable regulation.

## S2 — Cameroon DGTCFM public-securities procedure (government-operated descriptive webpage)
URL: https://dgtcfm.cm/procedure-demission-et-de-remboursement-des-titres/
As inspected 2026-11-02: securities account debit requires signed instruction by holder or authorized representative; CRCT initiates redemption/coupon payments, sets schedules, sends maturity reminder to Treasury 15 days before maturity; Treasury is described as obliged to fund full payment; in case of insufficient provision, CRCT notifies default to SVTs/Treasury and prepares a high-priority SYGMA order including principal and penalties. **What is established:** publicly described operational roles and possible failed-payment path. **Not established:** live participant contract, current scheme message schema, finality law, bank-specific authorization, actual settlement, penalty applicability to any specific instrument, or secured-repayment regulatory eligibility.

## Authority ranking
1. Issuer-authenticated, complete, effective-dated operative text plus amendments and official publication;
2. bank-specific signed regulatory permissions and approved methodology;
3. official issuer explanatory material and operational public guidance (context only);
4. third-party transcription (research lead only).
No level 3/4 evidence may silently satisfy a level 1/2 requirement.
