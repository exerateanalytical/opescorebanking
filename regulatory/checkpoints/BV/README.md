## New evidence
A complete six-page **third-party** PDF reproduction of COBAC R-2019/02 (23 September 2019) was visually inspected. Historical capital, payment-volume, safeguarding, incident, outsourcing and accounting clauses were mapped without authorizing executable thresholds. BEAC's official 2024 COBAC report reproduces **R-2024/01** in full, which covers banks, EMFs and payment institutions and broadens significant-incident triggers. Its explanatory text calls 2026 dates entry into force, whereas reproduced Article 41 gives 1 January 2025 plus 12/18-month transitional periods. Treat this as a legal-date terminology conflict, not a license to pick the convenient date.
