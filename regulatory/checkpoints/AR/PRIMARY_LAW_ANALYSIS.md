# AR — Primary-law publication and effect analysis (research, not legal opinion)
**New official primary source inspected on 2026-11-01:** Revised CEMAC Treaty, https://cemac.int/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/traite-revise-de-la-CEMAC.pdf .
## Text-level findings with locators
- **Article 40:** the Council of Ministers and UMAC Ministerial Committee may adopt regulations, framework regulations and directives, and make decisions/recommendations. This is an instrument-type/issuer gate, not proof that any particular 2025 regulation is complete or published.
- **Article 41:** regulations have general scope, bind in all elements and apply directly in member states. This prevents an assumption that a member state's local publication or interpretation can simply override the community text; specific conflicts still require qualified counsel.
- **Article 43:** regulations are published in the Community Official Bulletin and also in member-state Official Journals; entry into force is the date they specify or, absent such date, the twentieth day following publication. **Publication provenance, text completeness and any specified date must be independently recorded**. The provision does not itself prove the actual bulletin issue or date for 01/25 or 02/25.
- **Article 44:** subject to Article 43, acts of community institutions are applied in each state notwithstanding contrary national legislation. Legal applicability and the effect of a judicial order still require counsel and authentic records.
## Newly identified control gap: publication chain
Earlier AQ concentrated on the missing printed page 10, court cases and contradictory inactivity thresholds. AR additionally requires a **certified issue/page reference from the CEMAC Bulletin Officiel and relevant national Journal Officiel publication** for each instrument, together with a complete signed copy and any amendments/errata. A secondary article asserting “entered into force on 1 September 2025” is a lead, not an authenticated publication chain.
Do **not** infer that either regulation is invalid merely because this research has not located the Bulletin Officiel; absence of accessible evidence is not evidence of legal non-publication.
## 2026 consultation
The official COBAC communiqué of 2026-06-24 (previously verified AP/AO) described proposed implementing modalities for future adoption, distinct from existing obligations. This run did not obtain a signed final adopted instrument or certified no-adoption confirmation. **No proposed implementation modality becomes executable.**
## Litigation
Separate claims relating to 01/25 and 02/25 remain only secondary leads; case IDs, orders and enforceability are unverified. A filing is not proof of an operative stay. Do not use Treaty Article 45's rule on pecuniary *decisions* as an automatic execution mechanism for dormant-account *regulations*.
## Outcome
Legal source and effect assessment: OPEN. Proposed technical authority gates are design controls, not determinations of the law. External qualified CEMAC counsel and bank compliance must approve any legal interpretation.
