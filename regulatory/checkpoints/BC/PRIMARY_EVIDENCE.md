## E-BC-002 — COBAC 2024 Annual Report, official BEAC-hosted annex
- URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Rapport-annuel-de-la-COBAC-2024-PDF-14.8-Mo_compressed-1.pdf
- Passage: report printed pp. 78–79 (summary) and annex pp. 107–122, R-2024/01; web text lines 4259–4540.
- Article 16: all institutional data must be **available in the State of its principal headquarters in CEMAC**; do not infer a complete international transfer/hosting authorization model from this clause alone.
- Articles 22–25: access lifecycle, least privilege, high-privilege supervision, strong authentication/remote filtering, environment separation and sensitive-data protection.
- Articles 26–30: anomaly detection/logs, incident response, periodic vulnerability/penetration tests and significant-incident reporting. Art. 30 includes specified triggers and reporting updates; do not deploy a hard-coded rule without confirming amendments and the target institution's applicability.
- Articles 34–36: IT disaster recovery, externalized continuity and annual failover/return testing.
- Articles 37–40: project approval, preproduction tests, change rollback, separated environments, data quality and mandatory data dictionary.
- Article 41: formal commencement 2025-01-01; transitional compliance 12 months for credit/payment and 18 months for microfinance. Summary at printed p. 79 describes 2026-01-01/2026-07-01 as 'entry' dates, apparently conflating formal commencement with transitional expiry. **Preserve both source passages; seek issuer clarification before assigning executable compliance deadlines**.
- Article 41 expressly repeals contrary earlier provisions, including named clauses of R-2008/01, R-2016/04 and EMF R-2017/06. No inference of wholesale repeal.
- Provenance tier: **official institutional report containing reproduced regulation text**; legal authentication/publication, full original separate signed PDF and binary custody remain open.
