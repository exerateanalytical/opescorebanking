## BX-S1 — COBAC R-2024/01 reproduced in issuer-hosted annual report

Source: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Rapport-annuel-de-la-COBAC-2024-PDF-14.8-Mo_compressed-1.pdf

- **Article 29 (report printed pp.117–118):** Annual vulnerability and penetration tests cover essential or sensitive activities/assets, with findings and corrective measures reported through governance and internal-control reporting. Evidence must distinguish *actual test execution* from test plans.
- **Articles 34–36 (printed pp.119–120):** Critical continuity plans express maximum acceptable interruption and data loss; offsite backups; outsourcing continuity provisions; at-least-annual disaster-recovery tests require actual switch to recovery resources, representative operation, then return to primary without losing data. A backup-restore demo alone is insufficient evidence of full operational recovery.
- **Articles 37–38 (printed p.120):** Pre-production functional/technical tests, change approvals, rollback/data-restoration capabilities and development/test/production separation.
- **Articles 39–40 (printed p.121):** Data integrity, classification, independent consistency controls and normalized enterprise data dictionary. For a bank, this is directly material to immutable journals, monetary arithmetic, account state and settlement semantics.
- **Article 41 (printed p.122):** Operative reproduction states commencement 2025-01-01 with 12-month transition for existing credit/payment institutions and 18-month for existing microfinance institutions. Explanatory narrative on printed p.79 describes 2026 dates as entry into force. Possible transition-versus-commencement conflation; do not claim binding resolution.

Authority classification: **issuer-hosted reproduction of signed operative text**, not separately acquired signed standalone file; current amendment history not authenticated. ## Financial-integrity boundary

A recovery exercise, regulatory incident notice, penetration-test result or dictionary revision must never create or reverse a journal. Financial finality and balance restoration require independent proof of durable postings and external settlement, with idempotent replay and separate reconciliation. All design proposals remain non-executable.
