# BZ — Evidence delta and provenance

## Source E-BZ-01 — issuer-hosted scanned Instruction 01/GR/2014
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/instruction_01_gr_du_17032014_surveillance_sp_cemac.pdf
Issuer: BEAC. Access: web PDF text and visual first/final pages, 2026-11-02. Status: issuer-hosted **historical scanned text**, not independently acquired/hash-verified or proven current.
- Article 1(b–c): adherent defined by signed SYGMA/SYSTAC agreements; subject includes BEAC and system participants. No bank agreement was obtained.
- Articles 2–6: surveillance scope, Article 3 historical exclusions (e-money, electronic money transfers, card systems, securities and government-securities systems), Articles 4–6 participant/SYGMA/SYSTAC/SWIFT scope. Interpret only in historical context; later law may supersede.
- Articles 8 and 10: CRSP participation opinion, surveillance standards and circular authority. These do not prove a bank is admitted.
- Articles 16–17: legal, technical, financial and operational surveillance; specifically data authenticity/nonrepudiation, liquidity/credit coverage, transaction reconciliation.
- Articles 18–20: documentary/on-site control; technical and functional reporting for SYGMA/SYSTAC/SWIFT/network. Article 20 expressly delegates **form and frequency** to a CRSP circular; none obtained.
- Articles 23–26: audit observations, corrective plans, injunctions and possible suspension/exclusion under participation conventions. Do not auto-impose sanctions from historical document alone.
- Articles 30–34: contacts, historical six-month transitional period, circular/amendment powers and effect on signature.
- **Source conflict:** final page visible stamped/dated `17 MAI 2014`; issuer-hosted 2019 Financial Stability Review and the charter refer to `17 MARS 2014`. File name includes `17032014`. The browser text also reads 17 May. Neither date is resolved; original Bulletin Officiel and issuer clarification required.

## Decision
E-BZ-01 is strong issuer-hosted historical **content** evidence, but not proof of operative date, current validity, bank participation, legal scope or conformance. Do not activate executable policy.
