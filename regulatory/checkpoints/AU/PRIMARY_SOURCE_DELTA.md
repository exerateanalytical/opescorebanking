# AU — Primary-source research delta and chronology (2026-11-01)
## Newly examined issuer-hosted primary communiqué
**AU-E01: BEAC press release dated 15 April 2025** (one-page PDF), https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/communiqu%C3%A9-de-presse-gpe-travail-CDC-du-15-avril-2025.pdf
Source publisher: Banque des États de l'Afrique Centrale. Source type: OFFICIAL_COMMUNIQUE, not enacted legal instrument.
The Governor chaired the third meeting of the CDC/unclaimed-assets working group in Yaoundé on 15 April 2025. The release states that a consensus emerged on the relevance and quality of the draft dealing with inactive accounts and unclaimed assets. It separately records that the two active CDCs opposed **total** COBAC supervision of their activities and preferred supervision limited to banking activities. Revised texts were to be presented to competent CEMAC institutions for decision. These are historical positions at a pre-adoption consultation stage, not legal rulings or proof of the final text's scope. The official statement does not establish that any proposed text was adopted on 15 April.

## Previously known official primary evidence, rechecked for chronology
**AU-E02: BEAC communiqué, 12 February 2025** (second working-group meeting), https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/communiqu%C3%A9-de-presse_001.pdf
The two proposals were still drafts; the supervisory options and unclaimed-assets modalities remained under discussion. The release referred to the previous meeting on 9 December 2024. This source was already in the programme's research history; its chronology was rechecked, not counted as a new enacted instrument.

**AU-E03: UMAC Ministerial Committee communiqué, 12 July 2025**, https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Communique-CMO-12072025.pdf
The Committee announced adoption of two regulations by subject: CDC supervision and inactive accounts/unclaimed assets. It does NOT itself supply complete numbered instruments, certified Official Bulletin issue/page, effective date, or judicial disposition. The adoption fact is already in Checkpoint AS; AU does not duplicate that as a new discovery.

**AU-E04: COBAC consultation communiqué, 24 June 2026**, https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Communiqu%C3%A9-final-consultation-de-la-profession_260624_183742.pdf
Page 3 discusses proposed regulations on CDC governance/prudential matters and on modalities for inactive accounts/unclaimed assets. It explicitly recalls existing obligations to transfer unclaimed assets within the periods prescribed by regulations in force, while saying that the proposed modalities will be specified by a future text and revised drafts submitted for adoption. This is not evidence that a 2026 proposal was adopted; equally it does not suspend 2025 obligations. This source was already reviewed in AO/AP/AR; AU rechecks the separation between layers.
