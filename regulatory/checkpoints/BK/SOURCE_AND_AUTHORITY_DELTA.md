# BK — Evidence and regulatory authority delta (2026-11-02)

## Read evidence (new beyond BJ)
**BK-E01 — R-2020/01**: A five-page issuer-formatted PDF reproduction explicitly names R-2020/01 as an amendment to R-2010/02 on division of risks. The amended Article 7 grants COBAC discretionary reduction of certain exposure quotities for specified prominent/nationally important counterparties, with an annually determined published list; the text additionally requires the **beneficiary credit institution** to have a financial situation satisfactory to COBAC. It does not create a blanket entitlement for every bank. The reproduced Article 1 also addresses exposure limits and aggregation, but current amendments and approved risk computation have not been exhaustively established. Source: https://www.kalieu-elongo.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/reglement_cobac_r-2020_01-dovision-des-risques.pdf (web text lines 0–76).

**BK-E02 — R-2020/02**: A different issuer-formatted PDF reproduction states that R-2020/02 governs appointment and remuneration of provisional administrators and liquidators. Its Article 7 addresses liquidator selection, not exposure quotities. Thus the R-2020/02 reference in D-2025/328 Article 2 is materially inconsistent with the actual subject and Article 7 of the referenced regulation. Source: https://kalieu-elongo.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Re%CC%80glement-COBAC-R-2020-02-nomination-administrateurs-provisoires-et-liquidateurs.pdf (web text lines 0–24, 62–76).

**BK-E03 — Government-hosted D-2025/328 reinspection**: The decision's recitals expressly cite R-2020/01 and the new Article 7; its operative Article 2 cites R-2020/02. The recitals state 37 eligible, but enumerate 11 Cameroon + 6 Congo + 10 Gabon + 2 Equatorial Guinea + 7 Chad = 36. The decision is dated 12 Dec 2025 internally; government catalogue metadata shows 2 Dec 2025. Source: https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/9493 (web PDF index lines 10–15, 29–31, 36–83). Government page metadata: https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/d%C3%A9cision-cobac-d-2025328-arr%C3%AAtant-la-liste-des-entreprises-de-grand-standing-et-dimportance.

**BK-E04 — independent corroboration, not legal authority**: A Dec 24 2025 industry report counts 36 entities, consistent with the enumerated roster, but cannot correct the 37 statement in the regulator's decision. Source: https://monde-infos.net/secteur-bancaire-en-zone-cemac-la-cobac-devoile-sa-liste-des-entreprises-a-grand-standing-pour-2026/.

**BK-E05 — independent cross-instrument classification**: A Banque de France 2020 report identifies R-2020/01 as risk division and R-2020/02 as provisional administration/liquidators, corroborating the reproduced instrument titles. Source: https://publications.banque-france.fr/sites/default/files/medias/documents/cmaf_2020.pdf.
