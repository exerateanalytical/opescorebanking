# Checkpoint BK — WP-ACC-005 | Regulatory cross-instrument provenance and fail-closed large-exposure relief
Date: 2026-11-02. Verified predecessor: BJ (GitHub `CONTINUATION_STATE.json` and contradiction register inspected). Phase 4 — Accounting Core / Financial Integrity. Last reported completed package: WP-ACC-004 (inherited, not reaudited). Active package: WP-ACC-005 OPEN/P0 BLOCKED.

## Distinct research contribution
The text of **two different 15 April 2020 COBAC regulations** was read through issuer-formatted reproductions (not authenticated issuer originals): R-2020/01 amends R-2010/02 on large exposures; R-2020/02 governs appointment/remuneration of provisional administrators and liquidators. This materially strengthens the earlier BJ concern: Article 2 of government-hosted D-2025/328 points to a regulation whose **subject is unrelated to large-exposure quotities**. R-2020/01 Article 7 (new) is the relevant textual candidate, subject to issuer correction and later amendments.

A further **bank-side condition** is explicit in the reproduced R-2020/01 Article 7: the credit institution seeking the favorable quotities must itself have a financial position judged satisfactory by COBAC. Mere counterparty presence on the annual list is insufficient to establish bank eligibility. The regulation also refers to COBAC discretion and annual publication; these are distinct from borrower KYC or ordinary customer categorization.

## Decision
Do not auto-activate 2026 preferential large-exposure treatment. Keep `D-2025/328` as unapproved reference evidence with a conflicting roster count (37 in recitals, 36 enumerated), conflicting cross-instrument reference, uncertain precise reduction semantics, unverified original bytes, no signed issuer rectification, no bank-specific authorization and no bank-approved mapping. A 2025 decision is not evidence of a 2026 grant; a 2026 decision is not evidence of a 2027 grant. Do not alter loan principal, collateral, provisioning, ledger, risk-weighted assets or settlement on the strength of a label.
