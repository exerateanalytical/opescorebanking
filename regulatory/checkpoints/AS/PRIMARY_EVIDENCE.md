Research date: 2026-11-01. No external request sent. Source PDF images were inspected via browser rendering. Issuer hosting and visible signatures/stamps support provenance at the public-publication level but are not certified instrument copies.

## E-AS-001 — UMAC 12 July 2025 communiqué: official adoption by subject
Official BEAC-hosted one-page PDF: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Communique-CMO-12072025.pdf
- The UMAC ministerial committee met in Malabo on **12 July 2025**.
- Its communiqué states that it adopted a CEMAC regulation on the conditions of exercise/supervision of **Caisses des Dépôts et Consignations**, and a CEMAC regulation on **inactive accounts and unclaimed assets** held by institutions supervised by COBAC.
- The document is signed/stamped on its face. This is **official confirmation of the ministerial adoption event by subject**.
- The communiqué does **not** reproduce the regulations, identify their exact numbering in the adoption sentence, provide the Community Official Bulletin issue/page, authenticate a complete 02/25 printed page 10, or establish any effective date or subsequent court outcome.
- Legal interpretation: adoption, official publication, entry into force, judicial effects and institution-specific implementation are **distinct propositions**; never infer one from another.
