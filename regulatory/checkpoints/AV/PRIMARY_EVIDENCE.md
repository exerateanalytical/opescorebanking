# AV — issuer-hosted accounting authority evidence (2026-11-01)
## AV-E01: COBAC Regulation R-2018/01
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/reglement_cobac_r_2018-01_relatif_a_la_classification_a_la_comptabilisation_et_au_provisionnement_des_creances.pdf
Publisher: BEAC official website, COBAC regulations catalogue. Type: issuer-hosted regulatory scan, **not** institution-specific approval; original binary not retained.
- Articles 1–3: credit institutions classify on- and off-balance-sheet exposures under the PCEC and R-2018/01. Article 2 explicitly says classification is independent of collateral/guarantees.
- Articles 4–10: healthy, sensitive, non-performing (immobilized, unpaid, doubtful); Article 10 counterparty/related-party contagion rule. The legal definition of the same beneficiary and any later changes require current-law verification.
- Articles 12–15: restructuring/rephasing authorization, conditions and probation; interest/fees on impaired exposures must not be recognized as revenue when unpaid, and earlier accrued income requires controlled reversal/reclassification under Article 15(e).
- Articles 16–17: source text states general provisioning annual minimum 0.5% on specified exposure base and 2% minimum stock of gross on-balance exposures, with the annual contribution rule ceasing at the floor. **Historical extracted values only, not activated.**
- Articles 18–23: specific provisioning depends on eligible collateral, age, coverage, and legal qualifications; Article 22 contains collateral valuation conditions. Do not flatten to one generic NPL percentage.
- Articles 25–28: classification attributes and period-end timing; COBAC can impose classifications; Article 28 requires prior COBAC Secretary-General approval for write-off/abandonment of claims on related groups/parties.
- Article 32 expressly abrogates contrary earlier provisions, notably R-2014/01.
- Article 33 states effective date **1 January 2018**, whereas signed decision is dated **16 January 2018** (visually checked last PDF page). Record this apparent retrospective date for legal interpretation; do not assume its operational legal effect or apply it to historical entries automatically.
- Article 30 restricts release of provisions previously held in excess of Article 18 amounts unless justified by actual recovery.
**Caution:** 2018 is not necessarily the latest rule as of 2026; later amendments, dispensations and institution-specific regulatory instructions have not been exhaustively checked.

## AV-E02: COBAC Instruction I-2015/01 (PCEC amendments)
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/instruction_cobac_i_2015_01_pcec.pdf
Official BEAC instructions catalogue; 14-page scanned document; cover and selected pages (1,2,3,6,9,10,12,13,14) visually inspected. Article 15 says effective upon signature; terminal page visibly dated **6 July 2015**.
- Article 1: amends PCEC under Articles 2–14.
- Article 2: divides account 191 into 1911 and 1912 and discusses general-risk provisions; historic numerical values also appear in later R-2018/01, which must govern source hierarchy where applicable.
- Articles 5–7: distinguish credit quality categories and restructure some chart-of-account segments; e.g., Article 6 lists 301 healthy long-term and 302 sensitive long-term, and Article 7 lists 321/322 for short-term quality. **These are historical candidate codes, not a certified 2026 master chart.**
- Article 10: subdivisions of current/checking accounts by risk quality (3711/3712/3713 etc.).
- Articles 12–14: off-balance commitments and credit classification attributes.
- Text references R-2014/01; R-2018/01 subsequently expressly repeals that regulation. Avoid blindly treating all 2015 explanatory text as current.

## AV-E03: COBAC R-2003/01 accounting organization
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/R-2003_01.pdf
Official BEAC-hosted text, Articles 4 and 10–14. Article 13 prescribes double-entry equality, documentary evidence/audit trail, chronological recording and standardized PCEC accounts. Source supports architecture invariants; current amendments and institution application still require validation.

## AV-E04: BEAC-hosted 2018 financial-stability review
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/RSF-AC_VF_27_03_20_1.pdf
2018 review, printed pages 48–49: describes the 2018 classification/provisioning reform and COBAC R-2018/01. Secondary official explanatory evidence only; not a substitute for the regulation.
