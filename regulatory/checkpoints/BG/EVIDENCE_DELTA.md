**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Predecessor:** BF, independently fetched from remote main. **Active:** WP-ACC-005, P0-blocked. **Nature:** non-executable legal/accounting research.

## Distinct verified evidence since BF
1. **BEAC/COBAC issuer-hosted scanned R-2009/01** was located and read across both pages: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/rgtcobacR_2009_01.pdf . Its Article 1 sets the bank share-capital minimum at **XAF 10 billion**; Article 2 sets financial establishments at **XAF 2 billion**. Article 4 contains a historic five-year transition beginning 1 June 2009 and milestones ending June 2014. Article 6 states application from 1 June 2009. This is original regulation text, not a current standalone authorization to apply the old thresholds in 2026–2029.
2. **Government-hosted R-2025/02 complete text** was re-read: https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/9490 . Article 4 says entry into force 1 January 2026; Article 5 requires full paid-up minimum for newly approved institutions; Article 6 gives pre-existing institutions a one-year transition, requires submission by 30 June 2026 of a capital-raising plan for those unable to comply, subject to Secretary-General approval, with conditional milestones through 2029; Article 7 continues R-2009/01 until 31 December 2026 for pre-existing institutions, then repeals it from 1 January 2027.
3. **New evidence distinction:** R-2009/01's actual Article 2 says **2bn**, correcting secondary commentary that said **1bn**. This does not change the previously registered BF thresholds, but strengthens authoritative provenance and supports a regression test preventing accidental adoption of a secondary baseline.
4. **Official COBAC 2025 annual report** remains listed by BEAC at https://www.beac.int/supervision-bancaire/publications/ but the linked 19,385,240-byte PDF could not be fetched by the browser (content-length limit). No conclusions drawn about its annexes or 2026 amendments.
5. Attempted direct PDF binary acquisition of R-2025/02 through container HTTP; failed with DNS resolution error. No local PDF, SHA-256, cryptographic signature verification, official Bulletin proof, or authenticated post-publication amendment chain is claimed. BEAC R-2009/01 was visually inspected via browser-rendered pages, but its original binary was not retained and hashed either.

## P0 interpretive question — not resolved by source juxtaposition
Articles 4, 6 and 7 of R-2025/02 create overlapping legal intervals for **pre-existing** institutions in calendar 2026: new rule in force, old rule continued, a one-year general transition, and an optional regulator-approved staged plan whose first milestone falls on 31 December 2026. **Do not assume that merely satisfying R-2009/01's 10bn/2bn thresholds is an unqualified 2026 compliance pass** or that an unapproved plan confers the staged schedule. Obtain legal interpretation from COBAC/institution counsel, with a signed institution applicability decision. Never apply a capital plan approved for one bank to another.
