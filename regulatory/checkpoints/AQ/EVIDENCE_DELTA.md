## New source lead: Article 26 transition
A 2025-11-13 press article https://www.newsducamer.com/avoirs-en-desherence-la-cobac-sollicite-le-minfi-pour-resoudre-le-differend-entre-la-cdec-et-les-banques/ describes an alleged signed 2025-10-22 COBAC–MINFI letter and quotes **part** of Article 26 concerning accounts already inactive for at least 10 years on entry into force. The quotation is **partial secondary reporting**; it does not reconstruct Article 26 or establish the original letter's authenticity. Its claimed effect and timing must not become an executable transitional batch rule. Obtain the original signed letter with annexes and issuer-certified printed page 10.

## Contradiction requiring explicit source precedence
A 2026-01-14 article https://invest-time.net/cemac-comptes-dormants-desherence-2026/ describes savings/vault inactivity as 5 years, conflicting with Article 4 of the inspected 02/25 reproductions (36 months). Do not average, choose the most convenient interpretation, or silently change a configuration default. Record as SECONDARY_CONFLICT, and require issuer-authenticated current text, any amendment and legal applicability review before an effective-dated rule can be activated.
