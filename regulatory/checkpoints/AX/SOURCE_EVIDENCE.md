# AX source verification and provenance
All URLs are issuer-hosted BEAC/COBAC documents. Sources inspected through web PDF extraction and visual page images on 2026-11-01. **No original PDF binary custody, hash or issuer-certified copy is claimed.**

1. **I-2003/01 in COBAC Bulletin no.6 (June 2004), printed p.187**, Article 2: the account listing assigns 2651 to *private* investment securities and 2652 to *public* securities; the following commentary says account 2651 records public fixed-income securities. The instrument is **internally contradictory**, not simply different from 2015. Source: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Bulletin-COBAC-n%C2%B06.pdf .
2. **I-2015/01, 6 July 2015**, Article 3 (printed p.2): 2651 is subdivided as *public* investment securities, 2652 as *private*. Article 8(b) creates 321 short-term healthy and 322 short-term sensitive; Article 8(d), printed p.7, incorrectly or inconsistently labels the parent 312 even though all children start 322. The error is visibly printed in the page image, not merely OCR. Article 15 says it takes effect on signing. Source: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/instruction_cobac_i_2015_01_pcec.pdf .
3. **R-2018/01**, Article 32 (printed p.11) repeals contrary earlier provisions, notably R-2014/01. Article 33 (printed p.12, visually inspected) states effect from **1 January 2018**, while decision clause is **16 January 2018**. The PDF text extractor dropped the '1er' but the page image shows it. Article 17 specifies the 0.5% annual general provision and 2% floor with a cessation condition when the floor is met; do not use incomplete rule fragments. Source: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/reglement_cobac_r_2018-01_relatif_a_la_classification_a_la_comptabilisation_et_au_provisionnement_des_creances.pdf .
4. **BEAC 2002 annual report**, page 201, states I-2002/01 entered into force 1 July 2002. This is a primary institutional *report*, not the original signed instruction. Source: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/rap-2002.pdf .
5. **BEAC COBAC catalogues**, reviewed 2026-11-01, list I-2002/01 and I-2015/01. Catalogues are not complete amendment certificates and do not prove no later rule exists. https://www.beac.int/supervision-bancaire/instructions-de-cobac/ ; https://www.beac.int/supervision-bancaire/reglements-de-cobac/ .
