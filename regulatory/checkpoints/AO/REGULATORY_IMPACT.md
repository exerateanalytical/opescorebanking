# Regulatory impact and fail-closed decision — AO

## Primary finding (24 June 2026)
The official COBAC Secretariat General communiqué from its 22–24 June 2026 consultation distinguishes existing obligations to transfer unclaimed assets under regulations in force from **draft** COBAC implementation modalities. The communiqué says the latter would be submitted for future adoption. This is a material regulatory-monitoring lead; it is not evidence that the draft has been enacted. See AO-SRC-001, p.3.

## Independent source-completeness check
The academic-hosted 10-page reproduction (AO-SRC-004) also skips printed page 10, jumping from Article 23 on printed page 9 to Article 27 on printed page 11. It does not repair the previously recorded gap in AO-SRC-003. Article 23's ending and Articles 24–26 remain unverified.

## Legal-status discipline
The supervisory communiqué weighs against assuming there is no transfer obligation, but it does not resolve separately the judicial status of 01/25 and 02/25. A press report on a pending case is not a certified judgment. Bank-specific legal counsel must resolve applicability, current orders, and jurisdiction before executable rule activation. No rule may assert blanket suspension, repeal, or finality from this evidence alone.

## Proposed requirements (not implementation)
- `REQ-AO-01`: Maintain separate effective-dated records for enacted CEMAC regulations, COBAC draft modalities, formally adopted COBAC implementing rules, supervisory correspondence and court orders.
- `REQ-AO-02`: A supervisor's draft notice cannot activate a financial transfer, accounting posting, closure or restitution policy.
- `REQ-AO-03`: An official statement of an existing obligation triggers legal/compliance escalation, not a software-imposed moratorium or unauthorized automatic transfer.
- `REQ-AO-04`: Require issuer-complete signed text, original court documents, legal applicability approval, institution profile, PCEC journal design and settlement proof before transfer implementation.
- `REQ-AO-05`: Separate dormant-account classification and reporting evidence from funds movement; classification alone never posts or derecognizes balances.
- `REQ-AO-06`: Unknown external outcomes remain unresolved and independently reconcilable; maker-checker cannot be bypassed.

## Financial, security and privacy invariants
No direct balance mutation; immutable double-entry journals and reversals; explicit clearing/settlement/finality; anti-duplicate idempotency; role separation; out-of-band verification of legal documents; no customer PII in external evidence requests; immutable source provenance; preserve all unknown outcomes.
