# BQ — Primary evidence, exact claims and limits
**Source BQ-S1 (issuer original, visually inspected 2 November 2026):** BEAC, signed two-page press release dated **12 August 2026**, https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/CP_MISE-EN-PROD-SYSTAC.pdf. Page 1 explicitly states SYSTAC2 production **since Monday 03 August 2026** and retail-payment scope (virements, prélèvements, chèques, cartes); ISO 20022 design; central and participant clearing compartments; disputes module; instant-payments module **for later production**. Page 2 explicitly describes SYGMA V10 as **being finalized** and expected in following weeks; this is **not** a certified live date.

**Source BQ-S2 (predecessor, issuer scanned primary):** BEAC Instruction 001/GR/2025 dated 17 November 2025, https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Instruction-N%C2%B0001-GR-2025_norme-ISO-20022.pdf. Annex has chronologically inconsistent milestone labels. BQ-S1 supersedes planned dates **only as to SYSTAC2's observed 3 August 2026 production**. The annex error and SYGMA actual timeline remain unresolved.

**Source BQ-S3 (predecessor, issuer scanned primary):** BEAC Circular 013/GR/2025 dated 19 November 2025, https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Lettre-Circulaire-N%C2%B013-GR-2025_portail-dacc%C3%A8s-MyStandard.pdf. CEMAC-BANK MyStandards is restricted; no participant-specific private schemas, conformance reports or approved access acquired.

**Source BQ-S4 (issuer public operational description):** https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/systemes-de-reglement-brut/ — SYGMA finality conditional on actual settlement account funds; not proof that V10 was live on 2 November 2026.
