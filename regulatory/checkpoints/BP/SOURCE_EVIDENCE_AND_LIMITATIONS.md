# BP — Issuer evidence, exact scope, and unresolved authority

## Primary issuer-hosted documents inspected 2 November 2026
1. **BEAC Instruction 001/GR/2025**, signed **17 November 2025**: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Instruction-N%C2%B0001-GR-2025_norme-ISO-20022.pdf. Scanned first page and pages 2–5 inspected through official-hosted PDF and visual screenshots. Articles 1–3 adopt ISO 20022 under the **ISO Accelerator Pack (IAP)** variant, cover SYGMA/SYSTAC interacting participants, and distinguish CBPR+ international, BEAC_SYGMA_ISO20022 and BEAC_SYSTAC ISO20022 domestic profiles. Articles 4–7 require participant migration to prescribed BEAC technical formats, provide support, allow clarification by circular and make the instruction effective on signature. The annex refers to MyStandards, vendor REST MT-MX interface and MX signature guide, but **does not supply those private contracts**.
2. **BEAC Circular 013/GR/2025**, signed **19 November 2025**: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Lettre-Circulaire-N%C2%B013-GR-2025_portail-dacc%C3%A8s-MyStandard.pdf. Issuer-hosted scanned PDF inspected. Establishes closed **CEMAC-BANK** MyStandards community with administrator approval, a **29-message** collection (ADMI/PACS/CAMT), examples and participant message testing. Requests participant enrolment within one month of signature. A list of types is not an executable message profile, and an online testing facility is not proof of qualification.
3. **BEAC SYGMA operational description**: https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/systemes-de-reglement-brut/. Central-bank-money gross settlement described as irrevocable and final **when the payer settlement account has available sufficient funds**. Technical participation prerequisites are described; no actual bank's participation verified.
4. **BEAC SYSTAC operational description**: https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/systeme-de-telecompensation/. Net clearing balances settle through SYGMA; public page describes a daily **13:00–13:30** window. This is not a transaction-specific receipt, current scheme SLA or finality timestamp.
5. **BEAC payment systems overview**: https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/presentation-generale/. Distinguishes direct, indirect and subparticipation and states adhesion agreements govern participation. Do not infer OPESWARE or any customer bank has such an agreement.

## External finality and accounting restrictions
- `pacs.002` (status) is not independently sufficient proof of irrevocable cash settlement. `pacs.028` is a status inquiry, not a payment. `camt.056` requests cancellation; `camt.029` is a response, whose approved semantics are not yet known. `pacs.004` is a return flow and must not mutate the original posted journal. `camt.053` / `camt.054` may provide account evidence **only after authenticated participant-specific rules and reconciliation**. Message labels are evidenced by the BEAC circular; their exact state semantics and legal finality require scheme rules.
- An order to SYGMA, even high priority, is not a settled outcome. SYSTAC clearing netting and settlement are different stages.
- Ledger posting must remain double-entry, immutable, exactly calculated, idempotent and institution-approved under PCEC. No financial status may independently create money.
