# BF — primary-source evidence delta and limitations
## Source BF-001 — official ministry-hosted COBAC R-2025/02 PDF
- **Official custodian:** Republic of Congo Ministry of Finance, Budget and Public Portfolio.
- **Document listing:** https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/node/4745 (date 10 Dec 2025; identifies attached PDF).
- **Original document URL:** https://www.finances.gouv.cg/fr/download/file/fid/9490
- **Read result:** Web PDF extraction returned complete Articles 1–8, adopted in extraordinary COBAC session in Libreville 10 Dec 2025, concluding with President Yvon SANA BANGUI's printed name.
- **Article 1:** minimum share capital of credit institutions with registered office in CEMAC.
- **Article 2:** bank category 25,000,000,000 FCFA.
- **Article 3:** financial establishment category 4,000,000,000 FCFA.
- **Article 4:** stated entry into force 1 Jan 2026.
- **Article 5:** institutions licensed after entry into force must have minimum *paid-up share capital* at establishment.
- **Article 6:** institutions active before entry into force get one-year transitional period from 1 Jan 2026; if unable to comply within it, submit a capital increase plan for Secretary General COBAC approval by 30 Jun 2026. Plan milestones: banks 14bn/18bn/22bn/25bn by 31 Dec 2026/2027/2028/2029; financial establishments 2.5bn/3bn/3.5bn/4bn by corresponding dates.
- **Article 7:** COBAC R-2009/01 remains in force through 31 Dec 2026 for previously active institutions and is repealed from 1 Jan 2027.
- **Article 8:** Secretary General responsible for publication and notification to monetary authorities, BEAC national directorates and professional associations.
