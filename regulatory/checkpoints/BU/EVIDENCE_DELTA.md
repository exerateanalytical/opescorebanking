# BU — Source provenance, citation conflict and limitations

## BU-S1 — BEAC research publication (issuer-hosted explanatory, not binding)
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Lettre-de-la-Recherche-N%C2%B08.pdf
Title: *Lettre de la Recherche*, N°8, first half of 2020; article by Emery Tchoussi Bah, then Head of Regulation, Standards and Methods at COBAC, printed pp.7–11. Printed **p.9 footnote 7** states R-2019/03 (23 Sept 2019) for prudential norms applicable to payment institutions. Browser PDF screenshot of zero-index page 7 visually confirmed the footnote. The article is useful contextual expertise, **not an operative legal instrument**.

## BU-S2 — BEAC 2019 financial stability review (issuer-hosted official report)
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/RSF-AC_19_VF-publish-ok.pdf
Printed **pp.43–44**, section 1.2.3: lists **R-2019/01** (payment service provider authorization/change), **R-2019/02** (prudential norms for payment institutions), **R-2019/03** (application/recovery of monetary sanctions), **R-2019/04** (publication of sanctions). Browser PDF screenshot of zero-index page 44 visually confirmed printed p.43, and searchable text on following page confirms R-2019/03 sanctions. This is stronger documentary evidence for correct subject attribution, **still not a replacement for operative text**.

## BU-S3 — BEAC electronic payment services report (additional official corroboration)
URL: https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Rapport-sur-la-situation-des-services-de-paiement-%C3%A9lectronique-dans-la-CEMAC_compressed-1.pdf
The official report describes R-2019/01 as licensing/changes and R-2019/02 as prudential rules for payment institutions. The search extract was examined; full binary not acquired in this run. This independently supports the R-2019/02 subject assignment, without establishing 2026 amendment status.

## BU-S4/S5 — Public regulatory indexes (not exhaustive amendment certifications)
BEAC payment index: https://www.beac.int/systemes-paiement/instructions-circulaires-reglements/
BEAC COBAC index: https://www.beac.int/supervision-bancaire/reglements-de-cobac/
The public listings show issuer-hosted Regulation 04/18 and BEAC Circular 004/2023. Absence of a newer item in an index **cannot prove absence of amendments**. A legal number without full issuer/subject/date is unsafe; similarly numbered instruments in different subjects must not be merged.

## Disposition
BU-C01: explanatory 2020 footnote inconsistent with official 2019 regulatory overview; record exact wording, reject R-2019/03 prudential binding mapping. BU-C02: number-only instrument references unsafe; do not automatically conflate separate 04/18 instruments. BU-C03: current 04/18, R-2019/01–02, Circular 004/2023 and bank-specific restitution applicability remain OPEN/P0. All prior COBAC/PCEC/settlement/judicial P0 remain open.
