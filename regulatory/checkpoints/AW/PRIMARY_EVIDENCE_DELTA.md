Important historical-context warning: the 1998 edition mentions a Cameroon accounting/fiscal year end of 30 June. **Never encode that as a current 2026 calendar requirement without separate current-law and institution verification.** Likewise its historical references to R-93/08 are not evidence of current internal-control law.
## AW-E02 — COBAC R-98/01
https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/cbR-98_01.pdf
Articles 1–4 institute the PCEC and define its scope and authority for implementation instructions. Article 5 gives historical introduction dates: Cameroon 1 July 1999; other CEMAC countries 1 January 2000. These are historical rollout dates, not 2026 legal activation dates.
## AW-E03 — COBAC Bulletin no. 6 (June 2004)
https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/Bulletin-COBAC-n%C2%B06.pdf
The issuer-hosted Bulletin reproduces **Instruction I-2003/01**, which expressly modifies the PCEC and I-2002/01. It changes, among other things, securities-account subdivisions 264/265. The Bulletin's explanation of R-2003/01 distinguishes banking-specific PCEC from general OHADA accounting rules. Do not use a generic SYSCOHADA chart as the banking PCEC.
## AW-E04 — I-2015/01 and R-2018/01 continuation from AV
https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/instruction_cobac_i_2015_01_pcec.pdf
https://www.beac.int/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/reglement_cobac_r_2018-01_relatif_a_la_classification_a_la_comptabilisation_et_au_provisionnement_des_creances.pdf
I-2015/01 modifies PCEC and cites R-98/01, R-99/01, I-2002/01 and I-2003/01. R-2018/01 Article 32 supersedes conflicting earlier credit-classification provisions, notably R-2014/01. Article 33 appears to set an effective date before its 16 January 2018 signature: preserve the legal question; **no retroactive journal rewrite**.
