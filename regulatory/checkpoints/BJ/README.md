## Material contradictions discovered within a government-hosted decision
1. Recital says **53 submitted / 37 eligible**. Article 1's named list has **36** entries: Cameroon 11, Congo 6, Gabon 10, Equatorial Guinea 2, Chad 7. The arithmetic 11+6+10+2+7=36 contradicts 37. **Never infer a 37th entity** or treat the recital as an authoritative complete membership count.
2. Preamble and recitals reference **COBAC R-2020/01** (amending R-2010/02 on risk division); Article 2 instead references **R-2020/02**. This is an intra-document instrument-number inconsistency. An external reproduction of R-2020/01 supports the preamble's subject, but legal correction requires issuer confirmation.
3. The government catalogue metadata labels the document **2 December 2025**, whereas the signed decision text identifies **12 December 2025**. Treat the signed-body date as the stated decision date, but preserve metadata mismatch and require official promulgation/notification verification.
4. The 2024 official COBAC annual report at section II.5 attributes complaints handling to **R-2020/01**. Separately, the government-hosted 2026 decision and a reproduced R-2020/01 identify that number with **risk division**; secondary sources identify complaints implementation with **R-2020/06**. Do not reuse the 2024 annual report's citation as an authoritative complaints-regulation ID without issuer confirmation.

Precise eligible exposure classes, calculation order, scope, legal-entity match, applicability and dates require legal/CFO/COBAC confirmation. No automatic calculation or exemption is approved.
